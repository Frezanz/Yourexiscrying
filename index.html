05 — RULES & REGULATIONS

Freedom to act.
Discipline to last.

A starting framework — the association should formally approve and edit these rules before treating them as official bylaws.

01Respect every member

No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or deliberate humiliation. Disagreements should be handled through discussion.

02Association before individual interests

BTYA resources, decisions and public representation must serve the association's agreed purpose rather than a personal agenda.

03Transparency in decisions

Important plans, money, responsibilities and committee decisions should be recorded and made accessible to members.

04Own your commitments

If you accept a task, communicate progress. If you cannot complete it, tell the responsible coordinator early.

05Protect the community's reputation

Members representing BTYA publicly should act responsibly and avoid using the association to spread rumours, hatred or misinformation.

06Change the rules together

Rules should be reviewed periodically and changed through the association's agreed decision-making process.

07 — FEEDBACK / REPORT

BTYA should be accountable to its members. Report a problem, flag something inappropriate, suggest an improvement or simply tell the association what is working.

Submissions can be made by members or visitors. Leaders should review them and record appropriate follow-up.
