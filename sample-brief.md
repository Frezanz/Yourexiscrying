# Sample editorial brief

**Topic:** 10-minute walks after meals and blood sugar

**SEO title:** 10-Minute Walk After Meals: Can It Help Blood Sugar?

**Meta description:** Learn how a short walk after meals may affect post-meal blood sugar, what research shows, and how to build the habit safely.

**Primary keywords:** post-meal walking, blood sugar, glucose control

**Structure:**
1. Walking after meals and blood sugar
2. Why timing may matter
3. What should readers actually do?

**Editorial standard:**
- Separate established guidance from emerging evidence.
- Prefer WHO, NHS, EFSA and peer-reviewed research.
- Avoid diagnosing or prescribing for individual readers.
- Make uncertainty explicit when evidence is limited.
- End with one concise, actionable reader takeaway.
